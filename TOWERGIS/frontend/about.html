About Beacon

Revolutionizing telecom infrastructure management through AI, transparent tracking, and seamless customer experiences.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide an integrated GIS-based platform that connects telecom infrastructure, customers, field operations, and administrative management through intelligent complaint handling, spatial visualization, and transparent service workflows.

👁️Our Vision

To build a smarter, more connected, and service-oriented telecom ecosystem where geospatial intelligence, real-time infrastructure information, and intelligent customer support work together to make network services more transparent, accessible, and reliable.

How to Use Beacon

  • Sign In / Register

    Click on the "Get Started" button to access the Beacon Customer Portal securely.

  • Ask Aira for Service Details

    Use the chat interface to ask Aira about tower coverage in your specific area (e.g., "Check service in Hinjewadi").

  • Log a Complaint

    If you face network issues, type "Register a complaint" in the chat, or click the "New Complaint" button to submit your issue directly to the telecom operators.

  • Track Your Status

    Visit the "My Complaints" tab or ask Aira to track your unique Complaint ID (e.g., CMP-001) to see live updates on your issue.

Our Journey

The concept for Beacon (formerly known as TOWERGIS) was conceptualized to solve the growing disconnect between consumers facing network outages and telecom providers lacking real-time localized feedback. Development began with the goal of integrating spatial data (GIS) with intuitive AI.

Officially launched in August 2026, Beacon transformed into a user-first platform, introducing "Aira"—our flagship AI assistant—making it easier than ever for everyday users to interact with complex telecom infrastructure data.
